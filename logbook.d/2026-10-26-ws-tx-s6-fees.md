### LB-NNN · 2026-10-26 · Data feasibility: Texas water/sewer and impact fees, first pass (Texas plan S6)
- **Question:** which Texas cities' water/sewer and impact fees can be coded from official pages that allow
  automated reading, so the fee scenario can use local fees instead of the Handbook $50,000 (D-034)?
- **What I did:** read robots.txt for Austin, Fort Worth, Dallas and Houston sites (Fort Worth and the Houston
  Permitting Center answer scripts with 403; Austin and Dallas allow reading); read Austin Water's impact fee
  page and the street impact fee page and coded them with verbatim quotes; wrote a fill-in table for the rest.
- **Inputs:** Texas data plan (S6, approved 2026-10-23); `config/fees/README.md`; D-034.
- **Outputs:** 11 rows in `config/fees/tx.csv` (10 Austin water/wastewater impact fee rows by plat-date
  schedule, 1 street impact fee row); `docs/research/ordinances/tx-fee-lookups-todo.csv`; ordinances README.
- **Findings:**
  - Austin's impact fee depends on the lot's plat date: $7,700 per service unit for lots platted after
    2023-10-01, but $800-$1,100 for in-city lots platted before 2007-10-01. Infill homes (ADUs, missing middle)
    sit on old lots, so the prototype is the pre-2007 Desired Development Zone schedule ($1,100).
  - Austin's street impact fee is $0 for an ADU or small redevelopment on a previously developed site (under
    10 PM-peak-hour trips).
  - With a water/sewer fee coded, D-034 uses Austin's local total, $1,434.74 per home, instead of the $50,000
    prior. Parkland dedication and meter fees are not yet coded, so this is a floor.
- **Decisions:** Ben, 2026-10-26: keep Austin on its local fee total ($1,434.74 per home) under D-034 as written,
  stated as a floor until its parkland dedication and meter/tap fees are coded. Proposed for HANDOFF.
