### LB-NNN · 2026-10-20 · Study design: Missing-middle panel and D-024 pre-registration
- **Question:** Can the data support an estimate of how many extra 2–4 unit homes follow a missing-middle rule, and what exactly will be estimated, before any estimate is run?
- **What I did:** Built the D-024 panel builder (`build_panel`): every BPS place of 10,000+ people in MN, NC, OH and TX, 2010–2025. - **Findings:** Minnesota alone has one treated city (Minneapolis), so the panel pools four states: 7 treated places (2020: Minneapolis, Durham city and county; 2022: Raleigh; 2024: Austin, Houston, Cincinnati) and 553 comparisons. Unlike the ADU panel, place fixed effects are feasible: every treated place has 2–4 unit homes, and a fixed-effects Poisson model keeps 420 of 560 places (73% of place-years are zero). With only coded places as comparisons there are none, so D-024 proposes counting uncoded places as untreated, a stated deviation from D-022.
- **Decisions:** D-024 proposed (for Ben): pooled panel; uncoded places as comparisons; PPML with place and state × year fixed effects, three event-time bins, two modifiers; NB random effects as the pre-specified fallback; NC and Minneapolis out-of-sample tests.
