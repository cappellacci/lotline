### LB-NNN · 2026-10-20 · Build: D-025 pooled hierarchical negative binomial for the ADU effect (pre-registered)
- **Question:** Can a partially pooled model estimate the ADU reform effect on a panel where 70% of places
  never record an ADU, and keep a common shock out of it?
- **What I did:** `src/lotline/model/pooled_adu.py`: negative binomial with normal place random effects,
  year fixed effects, eligible-parcel offset, event-time bands (0–1, 2–3, 4+) × dose, z-scored modifiers,
  rate × post, missing middle; maximum marginal likelihood by 31-node adaptive Gauss-Hermite quadrature and
  BFGS with analytic scores; separation (zero-support columns, iterated rectifier, collinearity after drops);
  loud guards; wild score bootstrap; randomization inference; D-023's checks; transport; robustness
  (fixed-effects PPML, Borusyak imputation with pairs bootstrap, binary dose). `scripts/dev/estimate_pooled_adu.py`.
  D-025 written in the analysis plan before any real run.
- **Inputs:** D-023 and its notes; Ben's approval (options 1 and 2).
- **Outputs:** the module, script, `tests/model/test_pooled_adu.py`, `docs/analysis_plan.md` (D-025),
  `docs/model_and_assumptions.md` (status).
- **Findings (synthetic):** quadrature matches brute-force integration (31 nodes; 15 were too few for skewed
  places); analytic scores match numerical ones; on panels with ~70% all-zero places the 90% intervals cover
  the band effects, θ, ρ and δ in ≥ 22 of 30 panels; under a −0.7 common shock and no reform effect, the
  overall effect's interval contains 0 in 87–97% of 70 panels (small negative lean ≈ −0.15). Smoke test of
  the real panel (counts only): 76 places, 760 place-years, 31 treated, 53 with no ADU in any year.
- **Decisions:** D-025 (Ben approved the approach; details pre-registered by WS-ENGINE).
- **AI:** Claude derived and wrote the estimator, scores and tests; no real-data estimate yet.
