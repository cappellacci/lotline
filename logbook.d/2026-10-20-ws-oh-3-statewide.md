### LB-NNN · 2026-10-20 · Data feasibility: Ohio statewide parcels, all 88 counties
- **Question:** Does the Ohio statewide parcel layer give a usable tier-2 denominator for every county, and where are the gaps?
- **What I did:** Ran `oh_parcels` for all 88 counties (per-county OBJECTID-range queries, centroids, place/township assignment), pausing once for disk space and resuming with the new `OH_PARCELS_VINTAGE` override so cached pages were reused. Fixed a validation failure: two counties (Clinton, Summit) export no `CurrentTo`, so their vintage is the snapshot date. Superseded the earlier Franklin/Hamilton snapshot of 2026-10-18 (533 entries). Profiled the processed table against the jurisdictions table and ACS B25024.
- **Inputs:** OhioStatewidePacels_full_view (snapshot 2026-10-19); cached TIGERweb ACS 2025 boundaries (no new census.gov requests); `processed/oh/jurisdictions`, `acs_geo_year`.
- **Outputs:** `src/lotline/adapters/states/oh/oh_parcels.py`, `tests/states/oh/test_oh_parcels.py`, `docs/data_provenance.md` (oh_parcels section only); `processed/oh/parcels/oh_parcels.parquet` (6,093,278 parcels; not in the repo).
- **Findings:** 6,093,278 parcels; 50.1% in permit-issuing places, 7.3% in permit-issuing townships, 42.6% on county remainder rows. Land use is unusable (>10% UNKNOWN) in 10 counties: Summit (no StateLUC at all), Muskingum (all 999), Meigs, Lucas (55%), Warren (44%), Coshocton, Hocking, Hardin, Fairfield, Cuyahoga (13%); so Akron and Toledo can't use parcel denominators yet. Elsewhere SF parcels track ACS one-unit detached homes closely (14 largest comparable places 0.88-1.01).
- **Decisions:** proposed: keep the ACS fallback for counties with >10% UNKNOWN land use until county crosswalks or CAMA data fill them.
