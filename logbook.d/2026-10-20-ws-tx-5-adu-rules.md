### LB-NNN · 2026-10-20 · Policy analysis: current ADU rules for Texas's 20 largest places
- **Question:** What ADU rules are in force today (and since 2010) in the 20 largest Texas places, so the ADU scenario (#162) stops treating almost every Texas place as "no rule coded"? Is there a 2025 state ADU law?
- **What I did:** Coded each place with the protocol §5.4 rubric, including the fields `scenarios/adu.py` reads. Places with no rentable ADU in single-family zones (only by rezoning, SUP or board case, or units that may not be rented) are coded explicitly as `adus_per_lot = 0`, which #164 reads as "none". Research ran in four parallel subagents plus Austin and state law myself; only opened URLs are cited, blocked sites were not worked around. Generator: `scripts/dev/tx_adu_rows.py`.
- **Inputs:** city codes and ordinances (official PDFs where reachable; the zoneomics.com mirror where municode, ecode360, amlegal or city sites returned 403), council agendas and minutes, news reports; Texas Legislature Online (SB 673).
- **Outputs:** `config/reforms/tx.csv` (21 new ADU rows, Houston and San Antonio 2023 rows extended), `scripts/dev/tx_adu_rows.py`, `tests/states/tx/test_reforms.py`.
- **Findings:** As of end-2025: Handbook-equivalent: Austin (HOME, 2024-02-05) and Houston (Livable Places, 2023-11-27; deed restrictions aside). Partial: San Antonio, El Paso, Plano, Lubbock, Frisco, Brownsville. None (explicit): Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Irving, Laredo, Garland, Amarillo, Grand Prairie, McKinney, Killeen. Not coded (no source opened): Corpus Christi, Pasadena. Texas has no 2025 ADU law: SB 673 passed the Senate 31-0 and died on the House calendar (2025-05-26).
- **Decisions:** Proposed conventions (for Ben): non-rentable or rezoning/SUP-only units count as "none"; a pre-2010 rule with no adoption date is coded at 2010-01-01 (rule_age a lower bound); `adu_max_sqft` blank when the cap is only a share of the main house.
- **AI:** Claude (orchestrator plus four research subagents) read the sources and coded the rows; every row is `single_coder`, and rows read only on mirrors or secondary sources carry "FLAG for second coder". Checked by the rubric tests and by running `current_rules` on the 20 places at 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025.
