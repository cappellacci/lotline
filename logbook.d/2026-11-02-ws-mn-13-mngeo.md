### LB-NNN · 2026-11-02 · Build: Greater Minnesota parcels from MnGeo's opt-in compilation (thread 13, M2–M3)
- **Question:** how much of outstate Minnesota can count eligible lots from real parcels instead of ACS, and
  does the statewide eligible table still keep one source per place (D-020)?
- **What I did:**
  - Listed each outstate county's use classes with statistics queries (no parcel download).
  - Wrote an outstate crosswalk (`mn_land_use_mngeo.csv`). It reads the property-class labels and the
    three-digit codes that Pipestone and Renville label, and leaves unlabelled bare codes UNKNOWN.
  - Checked each county's single-family parcels against ACS one-unit detached homes, with NC's 0.6–1.4 band.
  - Downloaded the 31 counties that pass (Ben approved), built `mngeo_parcels` on `metc_parcels.to_parcels`,
    set `parcel_sources`, and rebuilt `jurisdictions` and `eligible_parcels`.
- **Inputs:** MnGeo `plan_parcels_open` (layers 0 and 1); ACS 2020–2024 B25024; the metro parcel tables.
- **Outputs:**
  - `src/lotline/adapters/states/mn/mngeo_parcels.py` and `tests/states/mn/test_mngeo_parcels.py`;
  - `config/crosswalks/mn_land_use_mngeo.csv` and `mn_mngeo_counties.csv`;
  - `config/states/mn.yaml` (source entry and `parcel_sources`);
  - `metc_parcels.to_parcels` keyword arguments (metro behaviour unchanged);
  - `docs/disclosures.md`, `docs/data_provenance.md`, and the MN parcel rows of `docs/data_availability.md`.
- **Findings:**
  - Jurisdictions counted from parcels: 186 → 539. Population share: 64% → 86%. Tiers: 116/69/868 →
    130/408/515.
  - Every jurisdiction now has a denominator for 2010–2024, except Wyoming township and Baldwin, which had
    none before either.
  - Among the 353 newly parcel-counted places, parcel counts total 1.03 times ACS. 14 lake and farm townships
    fall under half their ACS count, because cabins and farm homesteads are not read as houses (the metro
    convention).
  - 18 opt-in counties send no use class. 5 send bare codes, and 53 of their places fail the 90% gate.
- **Decisions:** proposed:
  - the 0.6–1.4 county band for MN (as NC);
  - a place whose main county is not loaded stays on ACS rather than counting a slice;
  - eight places split between metro and outstate counties take the source holding most of their parcels.
- **AI:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5) built the inventory, crosswalk, adapter and hand-worked tests, and ran the
  download and rebuild. Each county was checked against ACS, and the places that moved were checked against
  their old ACS counts. No model run; no St. Paul outcome or post-2023 St. Paul roll was read.
