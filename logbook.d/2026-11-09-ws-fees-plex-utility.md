### LB-NNN · 2026-11-09 · Build: How duplexes and fourplexes pay for water and sewer (fee lookups, priority 1)
- **Question:** in the 21 places where only the house water/sewer fee was coded, is the fee charged per dwelling unit, per meter or by meter size for a new duplex and fourplex, and what does the building pay? Until now `fee_levels` charged the house fee again for every unit (`from SFD`).
- **What I did:**
  - Read the fee schedules already in `~/lotline-data/manual/nc_fees/` (Apex, Brunswick, Greensboro, Johnston, WSFCU, Wilson, Durham) and looked up utility rules, codes and fee pages for the other places. Four research subagents worked from official sources only, and a fifth re-opened the key pages to check the quotes word for word.
  - Coded 38 `U2`/`U3_4` water_sewer rows with `scripts/dev/plex_utility_fees_2026_11_09.py` (idempotent; validated with `load_fees`), covering Winston-Salem, Forsyth County, Brunswick County, Bend, Portland, St. Cloud, St. Paul (Met Council SAC), Austin, San Antonio and Dallas.
  - Where metering is the builder's choice, the central value is one meter per unit (the utilities' default) and the master-meter cases go in `amount_per_unit_low_usd`/`_high_usd`.
  - Regenerated `docs/research/fee-levels-vs-handbook.md`.
- **Inputs:** WSFCU utility rules (Part A Sec. 14(A), 42(B); Part B Sec. 128) and FY27 manual; Brunswick FY26-27 water/wastewater schedules; Bend Res. 3487; PCC 21.30.070, PWB FY27 SDCs, BES ENB-4.05; St. Cloud 2026 fee schedule and Sec. 575; Met Council SAC Procedure Manual (2026); Austin residential tap application; SAWS impact-fee meter-size chart; Dallas water/wastewater FAQ.
- **Outputs:** `scripts/dev/plex_utility_fees_2026_11_09.py`; `config/fees/{nc,or,mn,tx}.csv` (+38 rows); `docs/research/fee-levels-vs-handbook.md`.
- **Findings:**
  - Most places charge water/sewer per meter by meter size. A fourplex on one master meter pays well under the house fee per unit: San Antonio half an EDU per unit, WSFCU $919–$2,298 against $3,677.
  - St. Cloud gives fourplexes a 25% discount on WAC/SAC units ($4,650 against $6,200 per unit). Brunswick charges per bedroom, so a 2-bedroom plex unit pays $6,564 against $9,846 for a 3-bedroom house.
  - 11 places still have no row because their metering rule is in municode or another blocked source: Raleigh, Durham, Apex, Wilson, Johnston County, CFPUA (Wilmington, New Hanover County), Greensboro (duplex), Fort Worth, Houston and Salem. The fourplexes in Austin and Dallas are also undetermined.
- **Decisions:** proposed (Ben to record): where metering is the builder's choice and the code leaves it open, central = one meter per unit, range = master-meter cases. Metro cities whose Met Council SAC is an `OTHER` row get no type rows, because a type row would drop their city OTHER water/sewer rows.
