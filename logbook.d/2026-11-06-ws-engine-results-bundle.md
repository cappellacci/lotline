**Decisions proposed for HANDOFF**
- θ in the bundle is back-solved per draw from the engine's own additions (#310 Q1), so the page's product equals the engine at default sliders; `totals` are computed by `aggregate.homes_from_draws` from the same draws.
- The bundle applies a single-family completion factor and the displacement share (drawn from `aggregate.yaml`) on top of the ADU scenario's permits; earlier dev runs reported permits and homes before completion.
- State overlap flag = eligible-weighted median calibration distance: high ≤ 0.5, medium to 1.0, low above (Ben, 2026-11-06). A first rule (share of parcels outside the range) flagged MN "low" because the range is the West Coast reference cities (96–99% of MN, NC, TX and OH parcels sit outside it); the thresholds were set after seeing the five states (MN 0.65, NC 0.94, TX 0.69, OH 1.39, OR 0.11), so this is a display flag, not a pre-registered test. Re-basing the range on the fit set is a possible later entry.
- Only the ADU scenario is exported today; fees, missing middle and the stacked combinations are listed in `meta.scenarios_pending` until the engine runs them statewide.
