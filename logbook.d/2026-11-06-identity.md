**Decisions proposed for HANDOFF**
- Identity: warm paper neutrals, one brand blue (`survey` #2f6fb0 / #6ea3d6), one brass accent, square corners, hairline rules; the "lot-line rule" (rule + end ticks + square midpoint) is both the wordmark underline and the mark every result is drawn with.
- Fixed scenario colours: Baseline neutral, ADUs #2f6fb0, Missing middle #1a9e72 (green), Fee reduction #d1561b (vermilion), Combined full ink (dark values in tokens.json). Validated colour-blind-safe (all-pairs, both themes) with the dataviz palette validator.
- Compact mark: after reviewing Nolli-style and Mondrian-style options, Ben chose a block of six lots with ink lot lines and two `survey` lots on a diagonal (the infill), with lighter lot lines (4.5/100) and a heavier cut (7/100) for 16px. The design-system cover is built on the same plan.
- Typefaces: Public Sans (headings, UI), Source Serif 4 (report body), IBM Plex Mono (figures, code); all SIL OFL, so they can be committed. Wordmark set in Public Sans Bold and converted to outlines (the SVGs carry no font dependency).

**What Claude did and how it was checked:** Claude (Cowork, Fable 5.1) drafted the brief from HANDOFF.md, SUBMISSION.md and the WS-APP brief, then built the design system: palettes were chosen by hand and checked with the dataviz skill's `validate_palette.js` (categorical all-pairs CVD and normal-vision floors, ordinal ramp checks) and WCAG contrast computed for every text/ground pair named in the token usage notes; the wordmark SVG was generated from the Public Sans TTF with fontTools; every component preview was rendered headless in light and dark and inspected (two fixes: tier-scale swatch widths, cover relief blocks on dark). Ben reviewed the brief before the design session. Not yet checked by a person: the system as rendered on claude.ai.
