### LB-NNN · 2026-11-04 · WS-OR: Oregon SDCs for Portland, Bend, Salem (Eugene water only)
- **Question:** What do Oregon's larger cities charge in system development charges (SDCs) per new house, ADU and fourplex unit, and how close is that to the Handbook's $50,000 per unit?
- **What I did:** A Claude Code research subagent read the current SDC schedules on the cities' and utilities' own sites (Portland Water Bureau, BES, PBOT, Parks; Bend Resolution 3487 and Bend Park & Recreation District; Salem's FY26-27 schedule; EWEB) and drafted rows in the `fees` format, with prototypes of a 2,000 sq ft house, an 800 sq ft ADU and a 1,000 sq ft fourplex unit. Claude dropped six rows it couldn't price (Eugene's city SDCs: eugene-or.gov returns 403 to automated clients; Bend's ADU water and sewer, marked "na" in the schedule) and checked Portland's transportation SDCs against the live PBOT page in the browser.
- **Inputs:** sources listed per row in `config/fees/or.csv`; Handbook fee benchmark in `docs/research/lotline-causal-diagram-fees.md`.
- **Outputs:** `config/fees/or.csv` (39 rows), `tests/states/or/test_fees.py` (7 tests).
- **Findings:** Per house: Portland $35,885, Bend $34,598, Salem $23,798 (72%, 69% and 48% of $50k). Fourplex unit: $27,439, $25,610 and $20,663. ADUs: $0 in Portland (waiver, 10-year no-short-term-rental covenant) and Salem (exempt), $6,323 in Bend. All under $50k: for Salem's fourplex units and all ADUs, the Handbook's $25k cut exceeds the whole SDC bill. Portland exempts all new housing permitted 2025-08-15 to 2028-09-30 from SDCs (PCC 17.14.070.J): a fee reform not yet in `config/reforms/or.csv`; rows carry the full schedule. LOC's 2025 survey average is $18,048 per house ($13,135 in 2018).
- **Decisions:** none. For Ben: whether Portland's fee baseline uses the schedule or the 2025-28 exemption.
