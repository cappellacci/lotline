### LB-NNN · 2026-11-04 · WS-OR: Oregon state-law rule rows (SB 1051, HB 2001, SB 458, 2023 c.223, HB 2138)
- **Question:** Which Oregon jurisdictions did each state ADU and middle-housing law cover, from when, and with what rules, read from the statutes rather than memory?
- **What I did:** Claude Code (WS-OR worktree). oregonlegislature.gov and OLIS refused connections from our network, so the enrolled bills (SB 1051 2017, SB 458 2021, HB 2138 2025) and the HB 2001 session law (2019 c.639) were read from Internet Archive copies of the official files, and the current ORS 197A.420/425 and 92.031 text from oregon.public.law. A subagent extracted sections, thresholds and dates; Claude checked every threshold and quote against the extracted text. Metro's 24 cities were derived from Metro's RLIS District Boundary and City Limits layers (city centroid inside the district). `scripts/dev/or_state_reform_rows.py` writes one row per covered city, town or county remainder from 2020 Census population: SB 1051 ADUs (cities > 2,500, counties > 15,000, from 2018-07-01); HB 2001 ADU owner-occupancy and parking bans (2020-01-01); HB 2001 large tier (>= 25,000 and Metro cities > 1,000 plus the three Metro counties, all middle housing, by 2022-06-30); medium tier (non-Metro 10,000–25,000, duplexes, by 2021-06-30); 2023 c.223 small tier (non-Metro 2,500–10,000, duplexes, by 2025-06-30); SB 458 land divisions superseding each middle-housing row (from 2022-07-01 or later); HB 2138 (2027-01-01, large tier). 526 rows, no statewide row.
- **Inputs:** archived official texts listed in each row's `source_url`/`source_section`; Census 2020 P.L. 94-171 (`census_dec_pl`); Oregon Metro RLIS Metro_Boundary and City_Limits_poly layers.
- **Outputs:** `config/reforms/or.csv`, `config/crosswalks/or_metro_cities.csv`, `scripts/dev/or_state_reform_rows.py`, `tests/states/or/test_reforms.py` (19 tests in `tests/states/or/`: tier edges on a synthetic set at each threshold, supersede chains, local rows kept on regeneration, the committed file validates).
- **Findings:** The statutes don't say how population is measured; DLCD uses PSU estimates, so cities within 5% of a threshold may be misassigned (2,500: Rogue River, Lakeview, Turner, Myrtle Point, La Pine, Gervais; 10,000: Eagle Point to Silverton, 11 cities; 25,000: Newberg, Woodburn, Forest Grove). The 2023 session law (c.223) and 2018 c.15 (UGB wording in the ADU rule) were not read; those rows are tagged `[earliest-known]` or noted. Signature dates aren't in the enrolled texts, so `adoption_date` is blank for SB 1051, SB 458 and HB 2138. RLIS license is attribution-only (CC BY 4.0 / ODC-By compatible), and no pre-2018 RLIS taxlot archive is published.
- **Decisions:** none for HANDOFF. Effective dates for HB 2001 tiers are the compliance deadlines (model code applied after); early local adopters (Portland RIP 2021) get local rows next.
