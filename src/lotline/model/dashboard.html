Lotline engine runs

Results of Lotline's engine runs, read from each run folder's CSVs and run.yaml. Generated __GENERATED__. Two kinds of run: effect estimates (D-024 missing middle, D-025 ADU), whose effects are on the log scale (0.7 means about e0.7 ≈ 2 times the homes a place would otherwise have permitted); and calibrations (D-038 to D-041), which fit an uptake rate θ per 1,000 eligible parcels a year and test it on places and states it was not fitted to. Intervals are 90% unless noted.

About these runs

What each run estimated, on which data, with which settings, and how its results were read. The full specification is the analysis-plan entry named on each card.

Headline result

Effect runs: the overall effect, its interval, and randomization inference (how often reshuffled adoption dates give an effect at least this large; under 0.1 stands out from chance timing). Calibration runs: the uptake rate θ, the place-to-place variance α, and how the leave-one-place-out backtest did against predicting no effect at all.

Effect estimates by term

Each dot is an estimate and its line the 90% interval. Terms to the right of 0 raise permits; an interval that crosses 0 cannot tell the direction.

Checks

The pre-registered checks and whether each passed. A failed check means the main estimate may reflect something other than the rule.

Robustness: the overall effect under other choices

The same question answered with other estimators, samples or definitions. Reported, never used to choose. A result that holds only under one choice is fragile.

Randomization inference

Bars: overall effects when the reform dates are reassigned to other places. Line: the real estimate. If many bars sit at or beyond the line, the real effect is not unusual.

Leave one treated place out

The overall effect re-estimated without each treated place in turn. A wide spread means one place drives the result. Dashed line: the full estimate.

Out-of-sample tests

Places left out of the fit and predicted. Bar: the 80% prediction interval for the total; dot: what was observed. Pass rule as pre-registered (shown per test).

Added homes by treated place

Homes the model attributes to the rule in each treated place's post-adoption years (missing-middle runs). The ten largest, with intervals. Watch for one place supplying most of the total.

Uptake rate θ

Each dot is a fitted rate (homes per 1,000 eligible parcels a year at reference market conditions) and its line the 90% interval. Fits on one state alone, or without one state, show whether the states agree. Missing-middle rates count 2–4 unit buildings, so don't compare their size with ADU rates.

State totals, held out

Each state predicted from a fit that never saw it, then summed. Bar: P10–P90; tick: P50; dot: what was observed. Useful means inside the bar and within a factor of 2 of P50; inside only means inside the bar but further off; missed means outside. Log scale.

Place by place, held out

Each dot is one place: observed against the P50 predicted when that place was left out of the fit. On the diagonal is a perfect prediction; the shaded band is within a factor of 2. Hollow dots fell outside P10–P90. Log scale (0 is shown at the corner).

Test places

Places kept out of every fit and predicted once. Bar: P10–P90; tick: P50; dot: observed. Log scale.

Counts and the market term

What each state's total is under each count rule (runs that apply them), and whether places with a stronger market term really permit more (correlation of log observed rate with log market term; it should be positive).
