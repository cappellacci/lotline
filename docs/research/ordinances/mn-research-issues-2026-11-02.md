# Minnesota municipal housing research — open issues and audit (2026-11-02)

## State law check

Current Minnesota Statutes §462.357 gives municipalities zoning authority and permits cities to extend zoning up to two miles into unincorporated territory only where a county or town has not adopted zoning for that area: https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/462.357. The current statute does not include the broad ADU/missing-middle mandates proposed in 2024 SF 3964. The 2024 journal text is bill language and must not be cited as enacted law. Proposed 2025/2026 bills are not current law absent enactment.
