The drift makes the ranges honest; a gain in predictive power is not proven at the pre-set bar

D-090's forecasts were given one random multiplier per place for 2021–2025, its spread learned for each state from the other three. The bands now hold about 80% of places, as an honest 80% band should, and persistence with drift is useful in 3 of 4 states. The log score improves by a lot in total, but almost all of it is Portland; place by place, about as many get slightly worse as better.

σ is learned leaving each state out, so no state's own window sets its own band. With drift off, every place's draws and scores equal D-090's exactly (largest gap 3.6e-15). The median of d is 1, so each place's median is unchanged and the ×2 test cannot improve place by place.

Finding 1

The ranges now reach the observed totals

Without drift every bar is a sliver and misses. With drift persistence's range holds the total in all four states; its median is within ×2 in three (Oregon is 0.47).

Faint bars: no drift. Solid: with drift. Grey: persistence (F0). Blue: F1.

Finding 2

Most of the log-score gain is one city

Persistence: 17 places better, 22 slightly worse. The losses are small (the largest is Bend, −2.5): where the old band already held what happened, a wider band spreads probability away from it. Portland's score alone rises by about 270, because without drift the observed total was far outside a very tight band. Remove Portland and the gain is +158, still large, but the paired SE is built from these same uneven gains, so the 2-SE bar is not met.

Finding 3

Independent drift lifts a state's median when one place dominates its errors

Each place keeps its median, but a state's total is a sum of skewed draws, so its median rises. For F1 in North Carolina, the total's median moved from 830 to 1,493 (observed 790), because F1's σ learned without NC is the widest fold (1.43). This is why F1's state-total CRPS gets worse while its per-place CRPS improves.
