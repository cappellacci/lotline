Measuring distance from our own five states moves the ADU medians up and halves the ranges

D-060 moved the ADU calibration range from Portland, Seattle and Los Angeles to where the anchor's own fit places sit. You approved it on Nov 6 and it passed its gates, but its PR never merged. This run ports it onto today's fit set (70 places, 513 place-years) and reruns the five core states with the same seed. Only the six range values changed.

For your call

  1. Merge #519 on green? It changes the ADU numbers below. The live app keeps the old numbers until it is republished.
  2. Overlap flag thresholds. With the new range every core state reads "high" overlap. Keep the 0.5 and 1.0 thresholds as they are (my pick: they still separate a state like Wisconsin from the fit states), or retune them in a separate entry.

1The new range sits where our states are, not where the West Coast is

The range is the eligible-parcel-weighted 5th to 95th percentile of rent and home value over the fit place-years. The rate span is the same either way (2.96–6.81%).

2ADU additional permits: medians rise 26–30% in MN, NC and TX, double in Ohio

Five-year totals, 2027–2031, P10 to P90 with the median marked, on a log scale. The widening multiplies each draw by exp(s·z − s²/2), which keeps the mean but lowers the median and stretches P90. Less distance means less widening, so the median moves up toward the mean and the range narrows. Oregon barely moves because it already sat almost inside the old range.

3Means hardly move, so the expected number of homes is about the same

The sum of the five states' mean added permits goes from 9,689 to 9,728 (+0.4%). Additional homes net statewide: 3,848 to 3,885 (+1.0%). What changes is how wide the range is and where the median sits in it.
