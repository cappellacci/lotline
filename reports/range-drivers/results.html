Sensitivity · issue #54 · results of 2026-11-24

One uncertain number drives most of each reform's range

Every result is the spread of 1,000 model runs. For each run we know the values it drew for every uncertain assumption, so we can measure how much of the spread each assumption explains on its own. Nothing was rerun. Minnesota is the benchmark state; the other states follow it.

How to read this

Each bar is one state's range for one reform, split by what explains it. "Everything else" is place-to-place luck, which local rules get drawn, effects that only appear when two assumptions combine, and assumptions that explain under 3% each. The figure on the right is how wide the range is: the 90th percentile divided by the 10th. In Wisconsin, a state the model was not fitted on, most of the ADU range comes from its unseen-state adjustment, which is in "everything else".
