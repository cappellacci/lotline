Fixing how rules were coded makes places look less random

The coverage audit found rule rows that called "no ADUs" an allowed rule, read lists of every single-family district as three-quarters of a city, and read rural-only rules as citywide. PR #440 corrected 33 rows. This page refits A + place types on them and reruns the tests.

What this means

  • The fixes are improvements, not just changes: less unexplained place-to-place spread, narrower held-out ranges, and the same verdicts.
  • The engine's config is now two fits behind. It runs m_x 0.4 and core +1.20 from rows before the checks; the fixed rows give m_x 0.7, core +1.67, θ 0.240 per 1,000 and α 1.44. Moving config is your call.
  • The reform ratio stays unproven with six events.
