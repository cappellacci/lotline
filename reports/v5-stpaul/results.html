St. Paul built faster than the model said

After its 2023 reform allowed up to four homes on 55,269 single-family lots, St. Paul got 55 new 2–4 unit buildings in 2024–25. The forecast frozen before we looked was 9 to 28, median 15: a miss, at the 98th percentile. The model's mature rate looks right; its timing doesn't. It assumed the reform takes four years to reach half speed, and St. Paul got there in about one.
