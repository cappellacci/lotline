Lotline · Missing middle · D-092 run 13, buildings per project · 15 Nov 2026 (Minneapolis corrected 17 Nov)

Duplexes and fourplexes come in clusters

In the city permit files, a third or more of 2–4 unit buildings come in projects of 3 or more: 39% grouped by lot, 44% grouped by day. Clustering multiplies a place's year-to-year variance by 14.3 (by lot) or 5.6 (by day), far over the pre-registered bar of 1.5. By the rule, the project-based model goes back to Ben. The pre-run guess, that most projects are one building, was wrong.

Corrected 17 Nov 2026

Minneapolis rerun after a permit-file fix

Minneapolis's file had typed roof, remodel and addition permits on existing duplexes and fourplexes as new 2–4 unit buildings (#450, fixed in #451). Rerun with the fix, Minneapolis drops from 1,042 to 179 buildings (by lot); its 2017–2025 count is now 175 against Census's 165. Every other city is unchanged. Pooled clustering goes up slightly: by lot 12.8 → 14.3 with 34% → 39% in projects of 3+; by day 5.3 → 5.6 with 42% → 44%. The verdict is the same. The building-equivalent check (below) was not rerun; Minneapolis was not one of its large clusters.

Finding 1

Most cities are over the bar, and they differ a lot

Cities with at least 10 same-day projects, on a log scale. Minneapolis, Cincinnati, Cleveland, Medford and Portland sit near the bar (1.0 to 2.4): their 2–4 unit buildings mostly come one or two at a time. Austin, College Station, Wilmington and Columbus build them in large groups, which fits Texas's lumpiness from run 12.

Finding 2

"Same lot" wasn't a lower bound

The plan called same lot the low estimate and same day the high one. It came out the other way round in the pooled numbers (14.3 against 5.6). That's because one parcel can hold a whole build-to-rent community permitted over a year, while a same-day group catches only one day of it. Both are far over 1.5, so the verdict doesn't depend on which is right.

For Ben

Decided: record it, don't build it

Clustering is real and large, so it's the honest explanation for missing middle's wide ranges. A model of projects × buildings per project would match how building happens, but it adds no new information. Ben chose to record it as the reason for the ranges and a stated limitation (15 Nov 2026). Run 11 stands; the config choice is next.
