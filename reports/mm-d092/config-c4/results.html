Finding 2

Quote medians, not means

θ's range spans a factor of about 150, so a few draws with a large θ pull the mean far above the median (Texas: mean 39,580, median 12,066). The tool's headlines should give the median and the P10–P90 range. And because the reform reaches half its pace only after 4 years, a five-year window catches the start of the effect, not its full size.
