Treating each year as lumpy fixes the baseline's ranges; the three-year baseline stays

Eight pre-registered baselines were fitted on 2016–20 and scored on 2021–25, on 468 places the fit and tests never use. Letting each year's count be lumpy (projects arrive in bursts) beats one multiplier per place under every construction. The rule keeps today's three-year baseline, now with a per-year spread of φ = 9.1. Its ranges hold 79% of outcomes, where 80% is the target and runs 4–5 managed 37%.
