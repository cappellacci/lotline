Lotline · Analysis plan D-055 · re-score 2026-11-06 · not blind · refit adopted

Oregon with the refitted missing-middle rate

Before writing D-054's refit into mm_hazard.yaml, V9's Oregon prediction was re-drawn with it. The inputs, code and seed were the freeze's; only the parameters changed. Oregon's 2018–2025 building was already known, so this compares two parameter sets on seen data. It is not a test. D-038's draws reproduced the freeze exactly.

Finding 1 · By year

The refit stops the over-prediction after HB 2001

New 2–4 unit buildings a year across the 62 places reached before 2025. D-038's median climbs to 712 by 2025. The refit's stays at 218–252. Built counts ran 165–268 with no rise. Both share the 2015–2017 baseline (182 a year), which sits below what was built in 2018–2021, before any rule.

Finding 2 · Totals and scores

The first parameter set to beat no reform over 2018–2025

CRPS measures how far the whole predictive range sits from what was built; lower is better. Over eight years the refit scores 85, D-038 691 and no reform 279. For 2018–2022 the refit's range is too low (missed, under), because of the baseline.

Finding 3 · Single places

Place ranges are now too narrow

Across the 62 places, mean randomized P10–P90 coverage falls from 0.57 to 0.43; the nominal value is 0.80. With D-038, 21% of places built below P10 and 18% above P90. With the refit it is 21% and 34%. The state total is right, but single places vary more than the refit allows, mostly upward.
