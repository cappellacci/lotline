On the new rule rows, older housing stands out and owner share slips under the bar

We reran the D-083 place-factor screen unchanged, except that it now reads PR #417's ADU rule rows and the Form A multipliers fitted on them (mo 1.0, mx 0.7). The fit set grew from 395 to 491 place-years. Nothing in the model has changed.

For Ben

What this changes about the next step

  1. Housing age is now the clear candidate. Holm p 0.0005, α falls 28%, and no single place carries it (57 of 57 drops keep the sign and p < 0.10).
  2. Owner share still explains the gap between states, but no longer passes the within-state bar. Within states its Holm p is 0.117, just above 0.10. Pooled across states it is stronger than before: β -0.95 per SD and α falls 35% (was -0.81 and 26%).
  3. Recommended: one method entry that adds both to Form A, owner share as the between-state term and housing age as the within-state term, scored on the data tiers before any fit is read.
