Lotline · ADU engine switch to Form A + place types (D-088, D-089) · 14 Nov 2026

With place types, the Handbook ADU scenario adds two to four times as many homes in four states

The scenario engine now runs the model Ben chose in D-088: a suburb's ADU rate, a core-city multiplier (×3.3), the town prior from D-089, and partial local rules counting at 40% of the Handbook (m_x 0.4) instead of 100%. Oregon barely moves. In Minnesota, North Carolina, Ohio and Texas, the homes the reform adds rise by 1.8–4.1 times at the median, and the ranges widen. The split varies: the types and the new anchor make most of the rise in Ohio and Texas, and partial rules no longer counting in full make most of it in Minnesota and North Carolina.

Finding 2

ADUs under today's rules rise too, so the baseline is higher

The no-reform path rises in every state, most in Texas and North Carolina (about 45%), where core cities hold most of today's ADUs. With m_x at 0.4, partial rules make fewer ADUs today, which pulls this path back down and leaves more for the reform to add.

Finding 3

Where the ADUs come from, by place type

Core cities hold most of today's ADUs in North Carolina, Texas and Oregon. The reform's added ADUs come mostly from suburbs, where partial rules are common, except in Texas, where core cities add about as many as suburbs. Towns add from 2% (Texas) to 27% (Oregon) of what the reform adds: few ADUs today, but often no rule yet.

What it means

Every state's number changes; two pieces still need a look

These are the engine's own runs: judgment parameters, the anchor's lognormal draw, each place's own effect, extrapolation noise, the observed floor with its undercount draw, and now the core-city and town terms. Two things are approximations until they are redone for this fit. First, the anchor's judgment slopes (adu_anchor.yaml) are still D-053's. Second, the anchor's lognormal width comes from the profile interval, and only its correlation comes from the new fit's observed information. The D-089 label still applies: every state's range rests mostly on places unlike the ones fitted.
