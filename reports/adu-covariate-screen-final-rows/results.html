On the final rows both place factors pass, but owner share only just

The D-083 place-factor screen, rerun on the ADU rule rows as they stand in PR #419: after the source check and your ruling on four values. Form A's multipliers were refitted on these rows first: mo 1.0, mx 0.8 (90% range 0.7–0.8; it was 0.7 on the unchecked rows). Fit set 64 places, 491 place-years. St. Paul out, no unblinding.

For Ben

What this changes

  1. Housing age is unchanged and strong. Holm p 0.0004, α falls 30%, and every one of 57 leave-one-place-out drops keeps the sign and p < 0.10 (β +0.84 to +1.06).
  2. Owner share passes again within states, narrowly. Holm p 0.081 against the 0.10 bar, α falls 21%, robust to dropping any place (β -1.05 to -0.66). Pooled across states it is the strongest factor: β −0.99, α falls 36%.
  3. The pass rests on mx. With mx held at 0.7 on these same rows, owner share's Holm p is 0.110 and it misses. The source-check corrections alone moved it from 0.117 to 0.110; refitting mx moved it to 0.081.
  4. Recommendation unchanged: one method entry adding both factors to Form A, scored on the data tiers before any fit is read. Owner share should go in on its pooled role (the state gap), not on its marginal within-state pass.
