Lotline · ADU anchor draw terms for Form A + place types · 14 Nov 2026

Recomputing the anchor's slopes for the new fit moves state totals by a few percent

Each simulation draw shifts the ADU anchor θ and the place-effect variance α with the cost and market assumptions they were fitted at. Those shifts were still the ones measured for the old D-053 fit. Refit on D-088's 64 places with each assumption nudged, θ's shifts are nearly unchanged and α's are smaller. Statewide additional homes move by under 3.5% at the median and under 6% at P90.
